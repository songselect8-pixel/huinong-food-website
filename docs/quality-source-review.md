## Product-document topics

The product-type panels are questions for sourcing review, not a promise that all records exist. Frozen berries distinguish lot/form records and cold-chain evidence; dried fruit distinguishes drying/composition and dry handling; botanicals and tea distinguish plant part, blend declaration and finished bag scope. A COA is not assumed to cover every residue, metal, microbiological or virus topic. No testing laboratory affiliation or logo is asserted.
